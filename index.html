How to Play Shells & Feathers

Tic-Tac-Toe

Goal

Get three of your pieces in a row: across, down or diagonally.

Taking turns

  • Players take turns placing one piece on an empty square.
  • Player 1 always moves first. Choose whether Player 1 plays shells or feathers before the game.
  • If all nine squares fill up with no row of three, the game is a draw.

Against the Spirit

  • You are Player 1 and the forest Spirit answers each move.
  • Gentle picks open squares at random. Balanced usually finds the strongest move. Wise never loses, so a draw is a great result.

Two players

Share one device and take turns. The turn line above the board shows whose move it is.

Keyboard

Tab to the board, move between squares with the arrow keys, and press Enter or Space to place a piece.

How to Play Acorns & Leaves

Checkers

Preview: Acorns & Leaves can't be played yet. Its game engine is still being built. These are the rules the first playable version is planned to follow.

Acorns & Leaves is checkers (also called draughts) with a forest look: one player has acorns, the other has leaves.

Moving

  • Acorns move first, then players alternate.
  • A piece moves one square diagonally forward to an empty dark square.

Capturing

  • Jump diagonally over an opponent's piece to the empty square just beyond it; the jumped piece is removed.
  • If a capture is possible, you must take it. If the same piece can jump again, it keeps jumping.

Kings

A piece that reaches the far row becomes a king and can move and capture backward as well as forward.

Winning

You win when your opponent has no pieces left or no legal move.
